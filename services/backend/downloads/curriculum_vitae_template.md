# Curriculum Vitae Template
Standard academic CV structure for tenure-track assistant professor applications.

## 1. Education
Degrees, institutions, and dates.

## 2. Academic Appointments
Current and past academic roles.

## 3. Publications
List of peer-reviewed journal articles, conference papers, and book chapters.

## 4. Grants & Fellowships
Funding received, funding agency, and role (PI/Co-PI).
