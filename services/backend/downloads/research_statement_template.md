# Research Statement Template
This template is designed to help you structure your academic research statement for tenure-track applications.

## 1. Executive Summary
Briefly describe your primary research area, key contributions, and overall trajectory.

## 2. Completed Work
Detail your major publications, methodologies, and the impact of your research.

## 3. Future Work (3-5 Years)
Explain your future research plans, funding strategies, and potential student collaborations.
