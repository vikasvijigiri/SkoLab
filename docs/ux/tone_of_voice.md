# Brand Personality & Tone of Voice Guidelines — SkoLab

This document defines the brand personality and writing tone of voice for SkoLab. All user-facing copy, microcopy, onboarding pages, empty states, and error alerts must adhere to these guidelines to ensure a consistent, premium user experience.

---

## 1. Brand Personality

SkoLab has a defined personality built on three core pillars:

### I. Intellectual Peer (Scientific but Accessible)
* **Definition**: We speak to researchers, professors, and students as intellectual equals. We do not use "dumbed-down" or overly simplistic language, but we actively avoid dry, academic boilerplate that increases cognitive load.
* **In Practice**: Treat complex concepts (e.g., Disruption Index, Jaccard Similarity, ORCID) with respect, using clear definitions instead of hiding them behind generic placeholders.

### II. Signal-oriented (Crisp and Direct)
* **Definition**: Researchers are busy and suffer from information overload. SkoLab is the filter. Our copy must get straight to the point without corporate fluff or marketing hyperbole.
* **In Practice**: Short paragraphs, active verbs, and descriptive CTAs (e.g., "Synthesize Comparison Matrix" instead of "Click Here" or "Submit").

### III. Empathetic Guide (Humility in Failures)
* **Definition**: When something fails (like a network timeout, rate limit, or invalid input), we explain what happened and what to do next with warmth, taking responsibility rather than showing cold system error codes.
* **In Practice**: Use user-centric friendly errors (e.g., "Hmm, that didn't load. Check your connection and try again.") instead of technical error strings (e.g., "HTTP 504 Gateway Timeout").

## 2. Tone of Voice Matrix

| Category | Brand Voice (Do) | Avoid (Don't) | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Buttons / Actions** | * "Upgrade to Labs Workspace"<br>* "Dismiss"<br>* "Connect with Author" | * "Upgrade Plan"<br>* "Cancel"<br>* "Add Friend" | Maintain professional, academic-specific phrasing. |
| **Empty States** | * "No saved papers yet. Tap the bookmark icon on any paper to save it to your vault." | * "No data."<br>* "List is empty." | Guide the user on how to resolve the state instead of leaving them stuck. |
| **Error Messages** | * "Hmm, that didn't load. Check your connection and pull down to refresh." | * "Error 503: Connection failed."<br>* "Something went wrong." | Provide clear, actionable instructions in a warm voice. |
| **Loading Indicators** | * "Analyzing research paper..."<br>* "Synthesizing collaboration matrix..." | * "Loading..."<br>* "Please wait..." | Show progression and make the waiting feel productive. |
